2021 · Posters · Wall graphics · Game boards

About
the project

Kari’s exhibition grew out of a hundred days of isolation in 2021, when she revisited travel memories every day. I designed its visual language: the posters, the wall graphics, the game boards that guide visitors, and a set of fortune slips.

The posters turn a room into somewhere to travel: a swimmer in a cup of tea, a climber hanging from a line that leads to a bed. Five game boards invite visitors to look at photographs in different ways, and the fortune slips, 生活冒險實驗籤詩, offer small experiments for everyday life.
