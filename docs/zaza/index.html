ZAZA
Photobook

An interactive photobook about photography empowerment in rural Cambodia, bringing children's photographs and illustrations into a playful reading experience.

2019 · Photobook · Book design

About
the book

ZAZA runs photography education with children in rural Cambodia. This book gathers the children's photographs and illustrations, and invites readers to discover their perspectives through play.

Every spread includes a small game. Readers can follow the book in order or open it anywhere, and the children's drawings are woven into the layout. The front and back covers are printed on pearl paper.

Role — Book design, layout, collaborative concept development; the games are designed into each spread
Photographs and texts — ZAZA team

Reading through play

In one spread, readers cut along a dotted line and give a photograph to someone they like. In another, they are asked to draw their most treasured thing.

Book design and layout — Chian-Pei Yang
Illustration — Chian-Pei Yang and the children
Concept development — with the ZAZA team
Photographs and texts — ZAZA team
Photographs and illustrations — the participating children
