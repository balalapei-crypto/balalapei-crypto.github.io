About
the project

Soul Breathing, also titled A Guide to Finding Ease, is a personal project I began in 2024 and am still developing. It gathers my own explorations of how to slow down: each piece pairs an illustration with a short line and the name of a rune.

The pieces are shared on Instagram as they are made, so this page is a selection from work in progress, not a finished book.
