One-Forty
Voice of
Migrant Web

A photography website for the One-Forty migrant photography awards, originally called Voice of Migrants. Design and motion for a gallery that opens like a camera shutter.

UI/UX design · Animation design · Project management

About
the site

The website presents the photography of the awards online. It opens with a camera-shutter animation, then moves into a home page and a photo wall of the entries that scrolls like a contact sheet.

The concept was developed together with the team, and the copy was written by partners. I designed the interface and all of the animation, and managed the project. The site was originally called Voice of Migrants; the awards were renamed this year.

Role — UI/UX design, animation design, project management
